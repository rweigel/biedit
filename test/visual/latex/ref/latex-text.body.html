2 Spacing bf et al.

  1. a c (nothing between a and c)

  2. a   c (one hair space)

  3. a   c (one hair space)

  4. a   c (one full space)

  5. a   c (space after bf replaced with full space)

  6. a b c (shift for bold)

  7. a b  c (space replaced with nbsp)

  8. a b   c (last space replaced with nbsp)

  9. a  b c (full space added before b)

  10. a   b  c (spaces replaced with full space)

  11. a  b   c (first and last spaces replaced with full space)

  12. a  b   c (full space added before and after b)

  13. a   c (full and thin space added before and after b)
